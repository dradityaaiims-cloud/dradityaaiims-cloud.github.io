About

Aditya Singh, MD

I am an internal medicine resident at Saint Vincent Hospital in Worcester, Massachusetts, with a strong interest in pulmonary and critical care medicine. I trained at AIIMS, New Delhi, and PGIMER, Chandigarh.

I build these simulators because waveforms, loops and anatomy are hard to picture from a textbook. Every one is open source, tested and free, and I welcome corrections from anyone who teaches with them.

Find me on LinkedIn, Google Scholar, KevinMD and Medium.

How to cite

If you use a simulator in teaching or in a publication, please cite the version you used. Each one is archived on Zenodo with its own DOI.

    Send me feedback

    Ideas, corrections and bug reports all help. Tell me which simulator you used, what you expected and what happened instead, and for a bug, which browser and what you pressed. Never include anything that identifies a patient.
